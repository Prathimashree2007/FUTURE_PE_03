# SEO Content Pack

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an SEO content strategy designed for a local dental clinic in Bangalore, Karnataka.

The system uses a content cluster approach consisting of:

- One pillar article
- Four supporting articles
- Keyword research
- Search intent analysis
- Local SEO
- Internal linking
- Business-focused CTAs

The objective is to improve organic search visibility, build topical authority, educate potential patients, and generate dental appointment enquiries.

---

# 4. Content Cluster Structure

The content cluster follows a hub-and-spoke model.

The pillar article acts as the central hub.

The supporting articles act as narrower topic pages.

```text
                    PILLAR BLOG
                         |
        ---------------------------------
        |               |               |
        ↓               ↓               ↓
 Dental Implants   Teeth Whitening   Choosing Dentist
        |               |               |
        --------------- | --------------
                       |
                       ↓
               Emergency Dentist
```

The pillar links to relevant supporting articles.

Supporting articles link back to the pillar.

Relevant supporting articles can also link to each other.

# 6. Search Intent Strategy

The content cluster targets multiple stages of the user journey.

### Informational

Users looking for answers and guidance.

Example:

**How to choose the right dentist in Bangalore**

### Commercial Investigation

Users researching treatment options or comparing services.

Examples:

**Dental implant cost in Bangalore**

**Teeth whitening in Bangalore**

### Transactional

Users who may be ready to contact a dental service.

Example:

**Emergency dentist in Bangalore**

This combination helps the website reach users at different stages of the decision-making process.

# 8. Internal Linking Strategy

The pillar article links to all relevant supporting articles.

Supporting articles link back to the pillar.

Suggested anchor texts include:

- dental implant cost in Bangalore
- teeth whitening in Bangalore
- how to choose the right dentist
- emergency dental care in Bangalore
- dental care in Bangalore

The goal is to make navigation useful for readers while helping search engines understand the relationship between related content.
