# Search Intent Strategy

## Purpose

Search intent helps determine what users are actually looking for when they enter a query into a search engine.

The content cluster uses different search intents to reach users at different stages of their decision-making process.

---

## 1. Informational Intent

Users are looking for information or answers.

### Example

**Keyword:** how to choose a dentist

**Target:** How to Choose the Right Dentist in Bangalore

The article educates users about factors they should consider before selecting a dentist.

---

## 2. Commercial Investigation

Users are researching options before making a decision.

### Examples

- dental clinic in Bangalore
- dental implant cost in Bangalore
- teeth whitening in Bangalore

These users may be comparing treatments, clinics, costs, or benefits.

---

## 3. Transactional Intent

Users are closer to taking action.

### Example

**Keyword:** emergency dentist in Bangalore

A user searching this term may need immediate dental care.

The article should provide useful information and naturally guide the reader toward contacting a dental professional.

---

## 4. Navigational Intent

Users are looking for a specific website, clinic, or brand.

This type of intent can become more important once the dental clinic develops brand awareness.

---

## Strategy

The content cluster combines informational, commercial, and transactional intent.

This allows the website to attract users who are:

1. Learning about dental problems.
2. Researching treatment options.
3. Comparing dental services.
4. Looking for a dentist.
5. Ready to make an appointment.
