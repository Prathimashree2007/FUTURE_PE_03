**Intent:** Commercial

**Intent:** Commercial

Users are looking for information that can help them choose a dentist.

### 3. Dental Implant Cost in Bangalore

### 4. Teeth Whitening in Bangalore

**Intent:** Commercial Investigation

## Keyword Strategy

The pillar article targets broad dental-care searches in Bangalore.

The supporting articles target narrower topics with specific search intent.

This structure reduces keyword overlap and creates a connected content cluster.

Keywords should be integrated naturally without keyword stuffing.
