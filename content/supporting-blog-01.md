## H2: What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a treatment option used to replace a missing tooth.

An implant generally involves a component placed into the jawbone that supports a dental restoration designed to replace the visible portion of the missing tooth.

The complete treatment may involve multiple stages, depending on the patient's condition and treatment plan.

A dentist can determine whether implants are appropriate after examining the patient's oral health.

---

## H2: Why Do People Choose Dental Implants?

Missing teeth can affect several aspects of daily life, including chewing and confidence.

Depending on the individual's situation, dental implants may offer a way to replace missing teeth while providing a stable foundation for a restoration.

Potential considerations include:

- Replacing a missing tooth
- Supporting normal chewing function
- Restoring the appearance of the smile
- Providing a fixed tooth-replacement option

However, implants are not automatically the right choice for every patient.

### H3: Condition of the Jawbone

### H3: Type of Implant

Different implant systems and components may be available.

### H3: Type of Dental Restoration

The final restoration attached to the implant can vary depending on the patient's needs.

The materials and type of restoration may influence the overall treatment cost.

### H3: Diagnostic Procedures

Dental examination and diagnostic imaging may be required before treatment.

### H3: Additional Dental Procedures

Some patients may require additional dental treatment before or during implant treatment.

The need for such procedures depends on individual circumstances.
