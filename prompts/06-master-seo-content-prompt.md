# Master SEO Content Cluster Generator Prompt

## AI Role

Act as an expert SEO content strategist, keyword researcher, and content marketing specialist working like a professional SEO agency.

Your task is to create a complete SEO content cluster for a business website.

The strategy must focus on:

- Organic search visibility
- Search intent
- Keyword targeting
- Topical authority
- Local SEO
- Internal linking
- Helpful, human-readable content
- Lead generation

**Business Location:** [CITY, STATE, COUNTRY]

**Target Audience:** [TARGET AUDIENCE]

**Main Services/Products:** [SERVICES OR PRODUCTS]

**Primary Business Goal:** [BUSINESS GOAL]

For this project, use:

**Business Type:** Dental Clinic

**Location:** Bangalore, Karnataka, India

**Target Audience:** Adults, parents, students, and working professionals searching for dental care in Bangalore.

**Main Services:** Dental check-ups, teeth cleaning, dental fillings, root canal treatment, dental crowns, teeth whitening, dental implants, wisdom tooth treatment, and emergency dental care.

**Business Goal:** Increase organic website traffic, build trust and authority, and generate dental appointment enquiries.

# 1. Business and Audience Analysis

First, analyze the selected business.

Identify:

1. Ideal customer profile
2. Main customer problems
3. Customer needs
4. Main services relevant to search
5. Possible search behavior
6. Local SEO opportunities
7. Business opportunities for content marketing

Keep the analysis specific to the selected business and location.

---

# 2. Pillar Topic Identification

Identify one broad topic that can become the main pillar article.

The pillar topic must:

- Be closely related to the business
- Have strong relevance to the target audience
- Cover a broad subject
- Allow multiple supporting articles
- Match the business's services
- Have commercial or informational search value

For this project, use:

**Pillar Topic: Best Dental Clinic in Bangalore – Services, Cost & What to Expect**

---

# 3. Keyword Research and Search Intent

Create a keyword strategy for the pillar and supporting articles.

Identify:

- Primary keywords
- Secondary keywords
- Long-tail keywords
- Related/semantic keywords
- Question-based keywords
- Local keywords

Classify each keyword according to search intent:

- Informational
- Commercial investigation
- Transactional
- Navigational

Create a table:

| Keyword | Keyword Type | Search Intent | Target Article |
|---|---|---|---|

Do not use keyword stuffing.

Keywords must be selected based on relevance and user intent.
