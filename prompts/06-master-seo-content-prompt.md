# Master SEO Content Cluster Generator Prompt

## AI Role

Act as an expert SEO content strategist, keyword researcher, and content marketing specialist working like a professional SEO agency.

Your task is to create a complete SEO content cluster for a business website.

The strategy must focus on:

- Organic search visibility
- Search intent
- Keyword targeting
- Topical authority
- Local SEO
- Internal linking
- Helpful, human-readable content
- Lead generation

All content must be written specifically for the selected business and should not feel like generic AI-generated content.

---

# Business Information

Use the following business information throughout the SEO strategy and generated content.

**Business Type:** Dental Clinic

**Business Location:** Bangalore, Karnataka, India

**Target Audience:** Adults, parents, students, and working professionals looking for reliable dental care in Bangalore.

**Main Services:**
- Dental check-ups
- Teeth cleaning
- Dental fillings
- Root canal treatment
- Dental crowns
- Teeth whitening
- Dental implants
- Wisdom tooth treatment
- Emergency dental care

**Primary Business Goal:** Increase organic website traffic, build trust and authority, and generate dental appointment enquiries.

**SEO Focus:** Local SEO for dental services in Bangalore.

**Content Goal:** Create one comprehensive pillar blog and four supporting blogs that target relevant keywords, satisfy user search intent, build topical authority, and support lead generation.

All content should be written as if it will be published on the website of this dental clinic in Bangalore.

Do not invent the clinic's name, address, dentist names, qualifications, awards, certifications, reviews, exact prices, or other business-specific facts that have not been provided.

# 6. Content Cluster and Internal Linking Strategy

Create a clear internal linking strategy connecting the pillar article and supporting articles.

The pillar article should link to all relevant supporting articles.

Each supporting article should link back to the pillar article.

Supporting articles can also link to other supporting articles when the connection is useful and relevant.

For every recommended internal link, provide:

- Source article
- Destination article
- Suggested anchor text
- Reason for the link

Use natural and descriptive anchor text.

Avoid generic anchor text such as:

- Click here
- Read more
- Learn more

### Example

**Source Article:** Best Dental Clinic in Bangalore – Services, Cost & What to Expect

**Destination Article:** Dental Implant Cost in Bangalore

**Suggested Anchor Text:** dental implant treatment and cost in Bangalore

**Reason:** Provides readers with detailed information about dental implant treatment and related costs.

- Business type
- Location
- Target audience
- Services or products
- Business goal

The AI should then automatically adapt:

- Pillar topic
- Supporting topics
- Keywords
- Search intent
- SEO titles
- Blog outlines
- Internal linking strategy
- Local SEO strategy
- CTAs
- Long-form content

For example, the same prompt system could be adapted from:

**Dental Clinic + Bangalore**

to:

**Salon + Mangalore**
